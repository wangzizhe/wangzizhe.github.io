A Phone Call That Took Me Back to 2012

I had a phone call with my cousin this Monday.

She's been running her own company full-time since 2023, and over the past year, things have finally fallen into place. The business now has steady cash flow, a team of around ten people, and is growing at a healthy pace.

Calling her my cousin doesn't really tell the whole story, though. The way we met was pure coincidence. It was June 2012 when my cousin got married. He's from Jinan, Shandong, while his wife is from Liuzhou, Guangxi, so they held two wedding ceremonies, one in each city. My mother met my cousin's wife's cousin at the family dinner the night before the wedding and invited her back to our home. The next day she called me and said, "Your cousin's cousin is here." That's how we first met.

The first thing she said to me was how tall I was. I was very confused because I'm only average height. It wasn't until I moved abroad that I realized what she meant. Compared with most Chinese people I met overseas, I was actually on the taller side. Only then did I realize that people from Shandong are among the tallest in China.

Meeting her ended up changing my life in ways I could never have imagined. She introduced me to Couchsurfing, and together they changed the way I saw the world. Before that, my life plan was fairly conventional: study in Germany, return to China, and find a job at a German company. I didn't have many hobbies, nor did I have particularly ambitious goals. Looking back now, many of the changes in my life can be traced back to meeting her in June 2012.

I've always found her life fascinating. She worked in Beijing for several years, earned a master's degree in Spain, returned to China to join ByteDance, and was later sent on assignment to London. Throughout those years, she was constantly experimenting with different startup ideas.

Eventually, she came to the conclusion that a traditional career probably wouldn't lead to the kind of financial freedom she wanted. At the same time, while working at ByteDance, she spotted a pain point in the market that nobody seemed to be addressing. So in 2023, she quit her job and decided to build a company around solving that problem.

2024 was brutal. The startup was in its cold-start phase, and there were many days when she broke down in tears. Every day she had to remind herself to keep going and not give up. Then, in 2025, everything started to change. The company became cash-flow positive. Today, she has companies registered in several countries, a growing team, stable cash flow, and impressive profit margins.

Looking back, she's spent years pushing the boundaries of what she thought was possible for herself. Watching her journey has been a constant reminder that ordinary people are capable of achieving extraordinary things.
