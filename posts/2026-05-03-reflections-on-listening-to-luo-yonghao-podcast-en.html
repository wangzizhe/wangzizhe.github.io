Reflections on Listening to Luo Yonghao’s Podcast

Luo Yonghao’s podcast "The Crossroads of Luo Yonghao" is one of my favorites. He invites truly representative figures from a wide range of fields, and the breadth of those conversations is something I rarely see in any other podcasts. What’s more, each episode runs for several hours, and the content is very engaging.

Luo’s eloquence goes without saying, he’s easily among the best speakers in China. What impresses me most is the depth of his knowledge across different domains. His guests come from all walks of life, yet he is able to converse with them effortlessly, regardless of age or industry. You can clearly tell that many of the topics he discusses are not something one can grasp through last-minute preparation, but the result of long-term accumulation. I genuinely admire the breadth of his experience.

There is another thing I find interesting. Whether it’s Luo Yonghao or other top figures in their respective fields, I’ve noticed that they tend to stay very open to how society and technology are evolving, as well as to the perspectives of younger generations. I think this may be an important trait for anyone, especially in China, to become a leading figure in their field. The world is always moving forward, and people have to move with it. If one clings to the mindset of their youth and becomes set in their ways, it is difficult to adapt to a new era. In contrast, those who remain open and inclusive are not defined by a single moment of success, but continue to grow and shine over time.

I am also trying to become that kind of person.
