This Thursday, I listened to Zhang Xiaojun's interview with Lindon, the founder of Dyna, and something he said toward the end really resonated with me. When you're just starting out, there's not much to worry or feel anxious about. The hardest part comes when you're halfway through the journey, with no idea where the finish line is, no clue what the future holds, and no one else around. That's when you find yourself surrounded by darkness, and that's when things get truly scary.

I can really relate to that. I've been working on AI Agents for about six months now. I was incredibly excited at the beginning, but six months in, I feel like I've reached that "halfway point" myself, and I now understand exactly what he meant. But I'm not afraid. If anything, I still find the whole thing fascinating. To me, the most boring life is one where you can see exactly how everything will play out. Not knowing where the finish line is also means there's so much out there for me to create and discover. So I'll just keep doing what I've been doing.

Looking back at the past five years, there have been two moments that completely blew my mind. The first was when I used ChatGPT for the very first time. The second was seeing Tesla's flying car.
