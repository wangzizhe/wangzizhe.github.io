This week, there was a top-tier model release every single day:

  • Monday: Kimi K2.6
  • Tuesday: ChatGPT Images 2.0
  • Wednesday: MiMo V2.5
  • Thursday: GPT-5.5
  • Friday: DeepSeek V4

Kimi K2.6, MiMo V2.5, and DeepSeek V4 have all entered the top tier. On benchmarks, they are going head-to-head with Claude and GPT. On OpenRouter, their overall performance looks very close as well. So I gave Kimi K2.6 a try myself (and I’ll admit I’ve always liked Yang Zhilin). From my experience, it genuinely feels on par with the Claude Sonnet and GPT-5.4/5.5 models I use regularly.

The day DeepSeek V4 launched, Zhang Xiaojun Business Interview also released a 3.5-hour conversation with Luo Fuli. What interested me most was Xiaomi’s internal structure as a new entrant, and how strong MiMo really is. The interview covered exactly those topics. After listening, my biggest reaction was simple: fascinating. Her core team has only a little over a dozen people, with a total team size of around 100, most of them interns doing exploratory research. No deadlines, no pressure, no performance reviews, no hierarchy. It runs much more like a startup. Many people on the team had never trained large models before and learned as they went. Luo said that with the right environment, someone could learn in two months. The team includes PhD students and doctoral candidates, but also many recent undergraduates, and even college sophomores and juniors. She said younger people often think more freely and are less constrained by old assumptions.

She also said that when OpenClaw came out, she was deeply impressed. She used it all night until dawn and burned through more than $1,000 worth of Claude tokens. Two points she made really stood out to me. First, the future belongs to self-evolving agents and collective intelligence agents. Second, even if model capability becomes extremely strong, agent frameworks will still be necessary. I especially agree with the second point. A few years ago, LLMs were exciting, but that excitement felt more temporary and stage-based. In the age of agents, every day feels exciting. I strongly relate to that.

Then today, I started seriously using DeepSeek V4 Pro. It was genuinely stunning. After trying so many models: Claude Sonnet, Opus, GPT, Kimi, MiniMax, this was the first time I had that true “wow, this is the one” feeling, so I immediately canceled both my Claude and ChatGPT subscriptions. This afternoon alone, I used 50 million tokens and spent only RMB 10. For the first time, I felt that heavy AI usage could actually be affordable. Its performance is excellent, it is fast, and token pricing is unbelievably low. The whole experience made it hard to put down.

I increasingly believe that the capability gap among frontier AI models is now very small. What will matter most going forward is user experience and price. And after this round of use, DeepSeek V4 has left me extremely satisfied.

What a dazzling week it has been.
